## Stage Analysis: Market Structure

### Stage 2 (Advancing) — 2 symbols

**MONQ50, MIDCAPETF**  
- Perfectly aligned MAs (MA50 > MA150 > MA200).
- Price > all MAs; making new highs.
- Ideal entry zone: pullback to MA50 or breakout above resistance.
- **Bias:** Long swing trades, 1.5–4 week horizon.

### Stage 1 (Base/Transition) — 8 symbols

**MOALPHA50, JUNIORBEES, NEXT50IETF, ALPHA, ICICIB22, HDFCMOMENT, CPSEETF, MOMENTUM**  
- Price near or slightly above MA50; MAs bunched/slightly misaligned.
- **Character:** Repair phase or early breakout. Volatile; risk-on.
- **Setup type:** Pullback to MA50 or breakout above local resistance.
- **Bias:** Wait for confirmation; breakout traders preferred over pullback.

### Mixed/Transition (Stage 1–3) — 4 symbols

**HDFCSML250, MOSMALL250, MASPTOP50, MAFANG**  
- Price above MA50 and MA150 but MA50 ≤ MA150 (topping-style misalignment).
- **Character:** Recent strength but structure weakening. Momentum-driven, not trend-driven.
- **Setup type:** Best on pullback to MA50; avoid chasing breakouts.
- **Bias:** Medium confidence; ideal for mean-reversion plays.

### Stage 3 (Topping/Repair) — 5 symbols

**BANKBEES, SETFGOLD, HDFCGOLD, SBISILVER, and SBISILVER**  
- Price ≥ MA200 but below MA50, or MA50 < MA150.
- **Character:** Distribution underway; bounces are sells, not buys.
- **Setup type:** Wait for clear pullback to support + MA50 hold, then new-high breakout.
- **Bias:** Conditional setups; avoid for now unless support zone holds cleanly.
- **Note:** Precious metals (SETFGOLD, HDFCGOLD, SBISILVER) all in topping — signals sector distribution.

### Stage 4 (Decline) — 3 symbols

**NIFTYBEES, NIFTYIETF, MAHKTECH**  
- Price < MA200 and/or MA200 slope negative; MAs inverted.
- **Character:** Downtrend; no swing setup until MA200 flattens and base forms.
- **Bias:** Avoid. Reassess in 4–8 weeks.

## Key Risk: Broad Market Dependence

**All 22 analysed ETFs move with the underlying Nifty 50 / broader NSE indices.** The scan shows:

- **Nifty proxy (NIFTYBEES/NIFTYIETF):** Stage 4 decline (MA50 270, price 275 but MAs inverted).
- **Factor/small-cap leaders:** Stage 2 or solid Stage 1, BUT dependent on Nifty index not breaking below 270 (MA50 zone).

**Critical invalidation:** If NIFTYBEES closes below 270 on volume, all factor/small-cap ETFs will likely follow. Swing trade stop should respect this zone.
